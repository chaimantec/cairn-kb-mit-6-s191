# See also

Knowledge bases for neighbouring courses. Pass the `KB:` URL as `kb` to `kb_read` / `kb_list` to read
one; each states its own coverage, and none of them is part of MIT 6.S191.

- **MIT 6.7960 — Deep Learning** (Fall 2024; Phillip Isola, Sara Beery, Jeremy Bernstein).
  KB: `https://github.com/chaimantec/cairn-kb-mit-6-7960` — covers 15 of its 24 lectures.
  MIT's graduate deep learning course. Where 6.S191 lecture 1 introduces the perceptron, gradient
  descent, backpropagation and regularization in one hour, 6.7960 treats each in depth: matrix-calculus
  backpropagation, optimization and scaling rules, approximation and generalization theory. Start at
  [INDEX](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chaimantec/cairn-kb-mit-6-7960/main/INDEX.md).

- **MIT 15.773 — Hands-On Deep Learning** (Spring 2024; Rama Ramakrishnan).
  KB: `https://github.com/chaimantec/cairn-kb-mit-15-773` — covers lectures 1–2 of 11 only.
  An applied introduction covering the same ground as 6.S191 lecture 1 from a different angle: logistic
  regression drawn as a network, activation functions, loss functions, gradient descent, backpropagation
  with a handwritten worked example, and a first Keras model. Start at
  [INDEX](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chaimantec/cairn-kb-mit-15-773/main/INDEX.md).
